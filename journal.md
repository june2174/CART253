### Sept 22, 2026
First, since I had to create three different prototypes, I wanted to choose one specific subject and explore how I could change and reinterpret it in different ways. I usually like choosing themes such as space or the ocean, so I decided to use an alien as my subject. I also like pop and punk aesthetics, so I wanted to use bright, bold colors that would stand out.

At first, I only planned and created the representational and abstract prototypes. While working on the abstract prototype, I experimented with using a `for` loop to create the petal-like shapes around the alien. After making these two versions, I started thinking about what I could do for the weird prototype. I thought it could be interesting to combine the two different alien heads by arranging them alternately and creating a mosaic-like pattern.

Since I had already used a `for` loop in the abstract prototype, I also wanted to try creating and using my own function in the weird prototype. I thought the mosaic idea would work well for this because I could use a function to draw each small alien head and then use loops to arrange them across the canvas.

At first, I experimented with giving the alien heads random colors. However, because the random colors were being generated inside the `draw()` function, the colors kept changing every frame, creating an effect that looked almost like a disco light. I actually found the effect interesting and considered keeping it, but I thought it would be easier to clearly see the different alien designs at this stage. So instead, I created a function that draws each individual alien head and used `draw()` to arrange the heads into mosaic pattern.
